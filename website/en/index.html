It's daytime where you are

Miku liveson your desktop

From sunrise through dusk to late at night, the light in her room follows your clock. Pat her head, hold her hand, or talk with her; she'll remember what you tell her.

Free · Apple silicon Mac (macOS 13 or later) · Windows preview

More than a wallpaper

She walks around your desktop on her own. Move the pointer close and she'll look your way; poke her cheek or pat her head and she'll respond. Hold her hand and she'll follow your pointer. Pick her up by dragging her, then let go and she'll land back on the ground.

At around 1:00 a.m., she returns to her room, lies on her side in bed, and tucks herself under the covers. Say good night and she can go to sleep earlier.

On macOS, the app opens with Tda式初音ミクV4X, made by Tda. In Notebook → Settings → Character you can switch to the classic outfit or the Animasa model, or import a compatible PMX character.

On rainy days she carries a clear umbrella, at tea time she has a drink in hand, and now and then she fans herself, hugs a leek, or waves glowsticks. On holidays she wears a hat for the occasion. Put on some music and she rests a hand on her headset and moves to the beat.

Seven scenes, three times of day

The Shibuya scramble crossing, Ikebukuro, Akihabara, Shinjuku, a school hallway, a music room, and Miku's room. In Shibuya, crowds wait at the kerbs and stream across in every direction when the lights turn green. Rain and snow fall over the streets as cars and JR trains pass by; turn off dynamic effects whenever you want to save power.

Talk with her, and she'll remember

I'm so tired today.

You've worked hard today. Shall I hum something cheerful for you? ♪

  • Many languages.The interface comes in Traditional Chinese, Japanese, English, and Korean. She can reply in Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, English, or Korean, or automatically follow the language you use.
  • Sounds like Miku.Upbeat and direct, and she can't stop once the talk turns to music. She'll hum along, but never quotes copyrighted lyrics.
  • Your choice of personality.Cheerful, gentle, quiet, or one you write yourself, plus the name she uses for you.
  • Long-term memory.She remembers your preferences and the relationship you've built together. You can delete individual memories at any time.
  • Image understanding.Drag an image into the chat window when the model you chose supports image input.
  • Voice calls.Press the phone button and just talk. Speech recognition runs on your device, and you can cut in while she's speaking. Her voice comes from the local speech engine you set up.
  • Local or cloud AI.By default she uses a model on your own computer. You can also enter your own OpenAI-compatible cloud service and API key.

Her days

  • Special days.She remembers her birthday, Miku Day, Christmas, New Year, and summer festivals, as well as the day you first met. Magical Mirai, Miku With You, MIKU EXPO, and SNOW MIKU dates are checked through official sources every three days.
  • A notebook and a diary.Every night before bed, she writes about the day in her diary. You can read it in Miku's Notebook.
  • Song knowledge.200 verified Hatsune Miku songs are built in, each with its creator, year, and a link to the original. When she doesn't know a song, she says so. Tell her in chat what you'd like to hear and she can open it on YouTube or Spotify.
  • Memory album.Take a photo of Miku and the scene as they are, add a title and a note, and keep it as a memory you share.
  • Mini games.Rock-paper-scissors, a Vocaloid song quiz, and rhythm tap. Just ask in chat to start one.
  • Now and then, she comes to you.Turn on "Random little moments" and she'll hum, ask you something, or invite you to play at random times.

On your computer by default

By default, an AI model on your own computer handles each chat, and conversations, memories, diary entries, and images are stored on your device. If you switch to a cloud AI, your chat messages, memory summaries, diary material, and any images you attach are sent to the service you specify. Your API key is stored only in the system credential store (Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows).

The weather feature sends only the coordinates of the place you select. Checking official event dates and app updates only reads public pages. The optional online song lookup sends only the song title or the search text you enter, never the whole conversation.

Noticing the apps you use, the music you play, and the videos you watch, glancing at your screen now and then, and random little moments are five separate proactive companionship features. All are off by default and must be enabled individually. With a cloud AI, screen, app, and media information is sent only after you also turn on sharing.

Installation

macOS

  1. Download the app

    Download MikuDesktop-macOS.zip from GitHub Releases, unzip it, and move the app to your Applications folder. Tda式初音ミクV4X is included, so there is no separate model to download. To build from source instead, follow the GitHub installation instructions.

  2. Allow the app to open

    The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS may block it the first time you open it. Go to “System Settings → Privacy & Security” and select “Open Anyway.”

  3. Prepare your AI

    Local: install and start oMLX, then load a model. The default is Qwen3.5-0.8B; a larger model will give better responses if your Mac has enough memory. Cloud: have your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint (HTTPS required), model ID, and API key ready.

  4. Connect and start chatting

    Select ♪ in the menu bar, go to “Settings → Local AI,” pick the AI source, fill in the endpoint and model, then choose “Test connection.” Scenes and character interactions work without AI.

Windows Preview

The preview shares animations and scenes with macOS and includes the Animasa character. Desktop display, chat, and album photos have been verified on Windows 11; microphone and calls, Windows 10, and multiple displays are still being tested. Follow the steps below to build the unsigned executable.

  1. Get the windows branch

    Set up Windows 10/11 x64, the WebView2 Runtime, Rust (MSVC), and the “Desktop development with C++” workload of Visual Studio Build Tools, then clone the repository's windows branch.

  2. Build and run

    Follow the Windows guide (in Chinese): run windows/scripts/fetch-model.ps1 to get the fallback character, then run cargo build --locked in windows/src-tauri. You can import the Tda model in Settings by selecting the author's original ZIP.

  3. Prepare your AI and start chatting

    Install Ollama, download a model, and select it in Settings. You can also enter your own cloud endpoint, model, and key. Then open chat from the system tray or the desktop control bar.
